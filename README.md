# 🛒 BigBasket Products Analysis | Python & Exploratory Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing BigBasket product data using Python. The objective is to understand product distribution, brand performance, pricing patterns, discounts, customer ratings, and data quality.

The analysis includes data cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical analysis, outlier detection, and data visualization to generate meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze product distribution across different categories.
- Identify the most listed brands and product types.
- Analyze sale prices and market prices.
- Calculate product discount percentages.
- Examine customer ratings and missing values.
- Detect and handle duplicate records and outliers.
- Visualize pricing trends and category-wise performance.
- Generate actionable business insights.

## 📂 Dataset Information

- **Dataset:** BigBasket Products
- **Total Records:** 27,555 product listings
- **Total Attributes:** 10
- **Data Type:** Structured Dataset

The dataset contains product-related information, including product names, brands, categories, product types, prices, and customer ratings.

## 🛠️ Technologies & Libraries Used

- Python
- Pandas – Data manipulation and cleaning
- NumPy – Numerical operations
- Matplotlib – Data visualization
- Seaborn – Statistical visualization
- Jupyter Notebook – Data analysis environment

## 🔍 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading & Exploration
- Imported the dataset using Pandas.
- Examined the first few records.
- Checked dataset structure and statistical summaries.
- Identified data types and missing values.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Analyzed product distribution by category.
- Identified the top 10 brands by product listings.
- Examined product ratings.
- Compared brand-wise average ratings.

### 3. Discount Analysis
Calculated discount percentage using:

`Discount % = ((Market Price - Sale Price) / Market Price) × 100`

- Analyzed discounts for individual products.
- Compared average discounts across categories.
- Identified the most discounted products.
- Flagged invalid price records.

### 4. Data Cleaning
- Identified missing values.
- Checked and removed duplicate records.
- Removed records with invalid price relationships.
- Detected outliers using the IQR method.
- Replaced detected outliers with the mean of non-outlier values.
- Exported the cleaned dataset.

### 5. Data Visualization

Created visualizations to understand:

- Product count by category.
- Top 10 brands by product listings.
- Sale price distribution.
- Customer rating distribution.
- Average discount percentage by category.
- Average customer ratings by category.
- Sale price comparison across categories.
- Correlation between numerical variables.
- Relationship between sale price and customer ratings.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

1. **Category Distribution:** Beauty & Hygiene is the largest product category, accounting for approximately 29% of product listings.

2. **Brand Performance:** BigBasket's own labels, including Fresho, bb Royal, and BB Home, have a strong presence in the product catalogue.

3. **Customer Ratings:** Fruits & Vegetables and Eggs, Meat & Fish have no customer ratings in this dataset.

4. **Discount Analysis:** Bakeware, Steel Utensils, and Appliances have some of the highest average discounts.

5. **Price & Ratings:** Product price has almost no relationship with customer ratings.

6. **Sales Limitation:** The dataset does not contain sales volume or units-sold information. Therefore, actual best-selling and least-selling products cannot be identified. Product listing counts are used as a catalogue-presence measure instead.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- The dataset does not include actual sales or units-sold information.
- Approximately 31% of ratings are missing.
- Some price records contain invalid sale-price relationships.
- IQR-based outlier replacement can affect genuine high-value products, particularly because prices are right-skewed.
